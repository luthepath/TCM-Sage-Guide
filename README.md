# TCM-Sage-Guide
TCM Classics Knowledge Graph

### About TSG
5,000 years of history · 97 renowned physicians · 119 classic texts
TSG weaves the surviving literature of classical Chinese medicine — from the Huangdi Neijing, Shennong Bencao Jing, Nan Jing, Shanghan Lun, Jingui Yaolue and Mai Jing through to the classics of the Ming, Qing and modern era — together with the scholarly lineages of its physicians, into an explorable knowledge graph. You ask a question; TSG traces the answer back through the graph. Every response comes only from classical medical texts handed down to this day, with its source shown. Nothing invented, nothing guessed at — so that learners can see how Chinese medicine evolved over its long history.

* 📖 Read — canonical editions, OCR'd and laid out chapter by chapter, free to read in full, with search and plain-language explanation of the classical Chinese
* 🔍 Ask — put a question to a single physician or to all of them at once, and get answers drawn from that individual's — or the whole canon's — knowledge base
* 🧭 Lineage — see at a glance how physicians and schools influenced one another
* 🌏 Bilingual — 简体中文 / English

TSG builds a learning path connecting past and present for every learner of Chinese medicine.

# TCM-Sage-Guide
中医典籍知识图谱

### 关于 TSG
5000 年历史 · 97 位历代名医 · 119 部传世经典
TSG 把传世的中医古籍——从《黄帝内经》《神农本草经》《难经》《伤寒论》《金匮要略》《脉经》...到明清、近代经典——连同历代医家的师承脉络，织成一张可以走进去的知识图谱。你提出问题，TSG 从图谱里把答案追溯出来：每一条回答都只出自流传至今的中医古籍原文，并标明出处。不编造、不臆断，帮助学习者看见中医在漫长历史中的进化之路。

* 📖 阅读 —— 选择经典典籍版本进行OCR，免费阅读清晰分卷分篇的古籍全文，便捷的搜索、解释古文，帮助学习中医古籍；
* 🔍 对话 —— 对全体/单独医家进行提问，得到来源于个体/群体知识库的回答；
* 🧭 脉络 —— 医家/流派影响关系一目了然；
* 🌏 双语 —— 简体中文 / English

TSG为所有中医学习者修建一条古今相通的中医学习之路。
